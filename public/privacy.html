Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-26 · Applies to Speakco v0.11 on the web, iPhone and Android.

Speakco is made by [LEGAL COMPANY NAME] ("Tiny Hustlers", "we").

Your voice stays on your phone

When you practice, Speakco analyses pace, pauses, fillers, pitch and clarity on your device. Recordings are kept on your device so you can replay them, and are never uploaded, with one exception you choose: precise accent scoring (Pro) sends the short clip you are scoring to our server, which forwards it to Microsoft Azure Pronunciation Assessment and returns the score. Clips are not stored.

Speech to text

To show what you said, Speakco uses your phone's or browser's built-in speech recognition (Apple or Google). Their privacy terms apply to that transcription.

Camera

Video presence drills analyse eye contact and framing on your device. Video is never recorded or uploaded. The face model is downloaded once from Google.

If you create an account

  • Email and first name, to sign you in with a one-time code.
  • Your progress (profile, scores, practice history, without audio), synced so it's on all your devices.
  • Purchases: your plan and its status. Payments are processed by Stripe (web), Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never see card numbers. RevenueCat processes in-app purchase receipts for us.
  • AI coaching (Pro): the text of your answer and the scenario are sent to our server and to Anthropic (Claude) to generate feedback. Not used to train models.
  • Leaderboard (opt-in): your first name and weekly XP.
  • Bug reports you send: your note, a screenshot with typed text blanked, app version and device type.

Our servers run on Supabase. We don't use advertising or tracking SDKs and don't sell data.

Deleting your data

Account → Delete my account removes your account and synced data within 30 days. Data on your device is removed when you delete the app or reset it in Settings.

Children

Speakco is not directed at children under 13.
