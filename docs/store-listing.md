## Description
Speak so people listen. Speakco is a ten-minute daily speaking coach for work: meetings, interviews, presentations and
hard conversations.

• Live feedback while you talk: fillers ("um", "like"), hedges and apologies, pace, pauses, pitch and clarity.
• Meeting simulator: realistic questions from your own role, getting harder as you improve.
• Six courses, 36 short lessons: confidence, presence, storytelling, hard conversations, people skills, meetings.
• Word swaps: replace weak phrases with confident ones.
• Video presence: eye contact, framing and lighting checks with your front camera (video never leaves your phone).
• Optional accent coach for American, British, Australian or Canadian English.
• Progress you can see: streaks, a six-skill profile, weekly report card and challenges.

Private by design: your voice is analysed on your phone.

Speakco Pro: unlimited speaking rounds, AI conversations and coaching, precise accent scoring, and sync across devices.
Monthly or lifetime.

Pro; not stored); **Purchases** (app functionality). No tracking. Google Play Data safety: the same, encrypted in transit,
deletable in the app.
